# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Saved 2026-10-25 from https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/ and the site's public API. Check the site again before acting, because dates and event details can change.

## TL;DR

- **What:** NASA's free, 48-hour global hackathon. Theme: **"The Next Frontier"**.
- **When:** **Sat Nov 14 – Sun Nov 15, 2026.**
- **Where:** **Karachi local event (in-person)**, and we're registered. The venue and schedule are not posted yet.
- **Team:** **[Aero Snipers](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/aero-snipers/)**, up to 6 people. Karachi Local Lead: Adnan Baig. Members can join until Nov 15.
- **Challenge:** **Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots**. Details are in [challenge.md](challenge.md). Our game plan and current status are in **[plan.md](plan.md)**.
- **Big rule:** You **cannot start working on a challenge before the hackathon.** Researching data and tools in advance is fine. Writing the project is not.
- **AI use is allowed.** NASA calls it "Super Intelligence (SI)" this year. Your submission **must describe how SI tools were used**.
- **Cost:** Free. If anyone asks you to pay, don't pay. Report it to info@spaceappschallenge.org.

## Remaining steps

1. During the event, edit and submit the project from the **Project tab on the team page**. Submitting gets you a participant certificate and makes you eligible for judging.
2. Fill in the participant survey by Nov 30.

## Judging and awards

Local judges pick the **Global Nominees**. NASA and partner experts then choose the **Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions**. An executive committee picks **10 Global Winners**, one per award. These are the awards that fit our project:

| Award | What it rewards |
|---|---|
| **Local Impact** | Greatest potential for local impact (Punjab and Sindh crop burning, smog) |
| Best Use of Data | Makes space data accessible or uses it for a unique application |
| Best Use of Science | Best and most valid use of science or the scientific method (the harmonization method) |
| Galactic Impact | Most potential to improve life on Earth or in the universe |

Full list: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/awards/
